What we carry

Everything for a Bangladeshi kitchen, under one roof.

From fresh halal cuts to the exact brand of chilli powder you grew up with. If you don't see it, ask; we can usually order it.

Halal meat

হালাল মাংস

Chicken, beef, goat and lamb, zabiha halal. Cut to your order: curry cut, biryani cut, keema, bone-in or boneless. Call ahead for bulk orders for events and dawats.

Spices and staples

মসলা ও নিত্যপ্রয়োজনীয়

Shan, Radhuni, Swad, Ahmed and more. Whole and ground spices, panch phoron, rice, dal, atta, oils, pickles, ginger-garlic paste and kewra water.

Fresh produce

টাটকা ফল ও সবজি

Seasonal mangoes, lychees, bananas and the vegetables a desi kitchen runs on: onions, potatoes, green chillies, coriander and more.

Dates and specialty

খেজুর ও বিশেষ পণ্য

Premium Medjool dates by the box, coconut water, Horlicks, Bournvita and the imported essentials you can't find at the supermarket.

Snacks and biscuits

নাস্তা ও বিস্কুট

Cake rusk, tea biscuits, dal muth, potato crackers, motor bhaja and maamoul. The tea-time shelf that never stays full for long.

Desserts and sweets

মিষ্টি ও ডেজার্ট

Frozen Bangladeshi mishti (chomchom, kalojam, pranhara, kheersa), custard and falooda mixes, jelly crystals, sabudana and sago.
